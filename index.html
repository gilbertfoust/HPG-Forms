Who We Are

Building Self‑Sustaining, Equitable Communities

Humanity Pathways Global is a nonprofit and fiscal sponsor supporting leaders, projects, and organizations that confront systemic poverty and inequality in marginalized communities, locally and globally.

By combining education, economic tools, advocacy, and community infrastructure, we work alongside partners to unlock potential and protect human dignity for every person we serve.

Founded in 2022 and rooted in Detroit, our work spans multiple countries and collaborates with youth, grassroots initiatives, and global partners.

Our Pathways

How We Create Lasting Change

HPG focuses on four interconnected pathways that, together, create a cycle of opportunity, justice, and long‑term community resilience.

Education & Skill Development

From literacy support to vocational training and youth leadership programs, we expand access to quality learning that prepares people to shape their own futures.

Economic Empowerment

Micro‑enterprise support, financial literacy, and workforce pathways help families build stability, generate income, and participate fully in local economies.

Human Rights & Social Justice

Advocacy, legal support, and mental health resources address discrimination, exclusion, and barriers that keep communities from thriving.

Holistic Community Development

Community centers, health access, local governance training, and essential infrastructure projects strengthen the social fabric and shared wellbeing of each community.

Our approach is deliberately systemic: each pathway reinforces the others, so gains in education, income, and justice add up to lasting transformation rather than short‑term relief.
Next Generation Impact

Raising Global Youth Leaders

Through initiatives like Global Youth Leaders for Humanity, we equip young people with the skills, mentorship, and cross‑cultural experiences needed to lead change across continents.

Participants grow in diplomacy, public speaking, language learning, and community activism — becoming multipliers of impact in their families, cities, and nations.
